========================================================================
linux-acpi-region-access-excerpt.c.txt -- the same region-access path in
the kernel's own copy of ACPICA
========================================================================

Issue #1337 asks what the interpreter does with an unaligned `SystemMemory`
`Field` operand. This is the copy the Linux kernel builds: `v6.6` still
carries ACPICA under `drivers/acpi/acpica/`, and the repository's end
product runs on this interpreter. It is the same source as
`acpica-region-access-excerpt.c.txt` with kernel naming and kernel
formatting, so it is weak evidence that the two disagree and strong
evidence that they agree.

The fragments are the same decision points as in the ACPICA excerpt,
because that is the comparison worth making. Two things are *not* the
same and are worth naming rather than glossing:

  * This copy still has the October 2009 page-boundary clamp in
    `acpi_ex_system_memory_space_handler`; the ACPICA revision quoted in
    the sibling file has replaced it with a plain one-page cap. Neither
    checks the operand's alignment, and neither is reached here: the
    excerpts stop at the read switch.
  * `acpi_evaluate_integer` at the end is the OS layer, not the
    interpreter. It is here because it is the concrete statement of where
    a returned `Integer` comes back out of on this side of the boundary.

Same convention as the sibling file: `sed -n A,Bp` ranges of the real
upstream file, upstream's own line number as a prefix, re-derived and
diffed by `fetch-acpi-sources.sh`.
